The personal data operator is Roman Gennadyevich Zheltov, an individual applying the Professional Income Tax regime. This policy applies to the InteriorsEditor floor-plan editor in Telegram and to its web version. You can send a request to the operator at boumrz@gmail.com.

In Telegram, you open the editor through @InteriorsEditorBot on domiknote.ru. The standalone web version runs on www.domiknote.ru. You can use the editor if you decline repeat-opening statistics.

1. What happens to your floor plan

The project name, dimensions, wall and item positions, project versions and resources used are saved in the app’s storage on your device. When you use an available import feature, the app reads the selected file on your device. The server delivers the software and catalog resources. Editing and saving do not send your project, imported 3D files or backups to the server or the bot.

You can delete a project in the app after confirmation. Separate backups remain wherever you put them. If you choose a chat, cloud drive or another app from the system share menu, the selected recipient receives the file. The operator has no access to those copies. Clearing browser storage, changing devices or reinstalling the app may cause you to lose a local project. Save a backup first and check that the file is accessible.

When you expressly accept the current license agreement, the app saves its edition and the time you accepted it on the device. In Telegram the record is associated with the verified local account scope. In the web version it is associated with that browser. It is stored separately from the project and its backup. This lets the app administer the agreement and remember the edition you accepted. Changing devices or clearing data may require you to accept the agreement again.

2. Loading the app and verifying a Telegram launch

Hosting infrastructure accepts your HTTPS connection to deliver pages, the software and the catalog. The IP address, time of the request, requested resource and technical headers sent by the client are technically available at the network entry point. Delivery is necessary to take steps at your request to conclude a contract and perform it under Article 6(1)(5) of Federal Law No. 152-FZ. To protect availability and prevent misuse, the operator uses the necessary technical information within the scope of a legitimate interest and with due regard for your rights under Article 6(1)(7).

In the Mini App, the Telegram client provides signed launch parameters. To verify the launch on the device, the app uses the user identifier, time and signature. Depending on how you start the app, the package may also include a name, username, language, photo link and chat context. The Telegram SDK may save the original parameters in storage for the current WebView session. The editor verifies the signature and generates a derived code to open separate local storage for that account. The editor does not send the original package, name, photo or original Telegram ID to the app server or the current server-side Metrica service.

The purpose is to provide access to the selected account’s local projects and keep projects from different accounts separate on one device. The legal basis is performance of the license agreement at your request. The web version outside Telegram does not require this information. Signing in with the same account on another device does not transfer projects.

3. Opening statistics

Statistics help improve the app. When you enter the editor in Telegram, the app may send an empty request to count one opening. This counting is enabled by default. To avoid counting a reload as another opening, the app stores the time of the last verified launch to the nearest second in the current Telegram scope on the device. The statistics request contains no timestamp. You can turn off counting on the screen before you start or in Settings. Reading documents and viewing existing projects without entering editing are excluded. The web version outside Telegram does not send these requests.

The counting service hosted by Timeweb Cloud stores the daily number of openings in Moscow time. For openings in Telegram on iOS and Android, the current service also stores a total for each two-hour interval. The app selects the type of empty request according to the type of Telegram client. The service stores the interval boundary and the aggregate count. The statistics contain neither individual opening timestamps nor device model information. One statistics control governs both opening totals. The network node accepting the HTTPS connection can technically see the IP address and browser headers. These are absent from the counting service. The statistics request contains no visitor identifier, Telegram ID, name, project or project contents. Personal files, room plans and backups are excluded. The legal basis for processing technical information for this minimal counting is the operator’s interest in measuring editor use, taking account of your right to opt out under Article 6(1)(7) of Federal Law No. 152-FZ.

4. How we measure whether you open the editor again

Only with your separate permission does the editor use an initial marker on this device to count repeat launches in the current Telegram scope. The launch that sets the marker is excluded from repeat counts. The next separate launch may add one to the second daily metric. This method cannot link different devices or identify who opened the app. You can dismiss the request, decline or withdraw permission in Settings. You can still edit, save and export projects.

Your choice, the form edition, the action time and the previous launch marker are stored on the device separately from the project. They are not sent to Telegram CloudStorage, the operator or Yandex. The server receives an empty request of the relevant type. The additional aggregate is sent as part of a daily report with a shared service identifier. The legal basis for additional counting is your separate consent under Articles 6(1)(1) and 9 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ. You can read the terms before deciding. Consent to Additional Statistics.

Earlier versions used browser-based Metrica and server-side counting with a persistent account pseudonym. A setting could be saved in Telegram CloudStorage, and Yandex could receive individual visit events. The new release does not use earlier permission. Contact the operator about information already transmitted. Turning off statistics on the device stops new requests from the app.

4.1. Editor usage and errors

Separate permission in the Analytics settings allows the Mini App on iOS and Android to count launches in which an editor action or visible error of a particular kind occurred. The app sends at most one empty request per verified launch and code. The connection reaches Timeweb Cloud in the Netherlands. The counting service receives only the client type and one code from a fixed list. It keeps the Moscow date and daily totals by client type and code. The operator may send these totals to Yandex Metrica using a shared service identifier and may retain them to compare periods. The totals do not identify individual launches or their sequences.

The app keeps the choice, its edition and time, a launch marker and the codes counted in that launch on the device, separately from the project. The editor sends no Telegram launch data, account identifiers, project details, files, error text, stack trace or exact time of the action to the counting service. You can withdraw consent in the same settings or turn off all analytics. Either action stops future counting while the editor remains available. See the separate Consent for Usage and Error Analytics.

7. Processing methods, safeguards and retention

The editor automatically receives, verifies, transforms and stores information within the limits described above. The operator also reviews support emails manually. Depending on the purpose, processing may include recording, organizing, using, providing data to a specific recipient, updating, blocking, deleting and destroying it. The server uses HTTPS. Project scopes are separated on the device, original launch parameters are verified locally and summary reports contain only aggregate metrics. The statistics server excludes client headers from requests to the counting service. The operator does not publish projects or individual user statistics. The operator does not ask for biometric or health data to run the editor or make automated decisions based on statistics that determine user rights.

  • On the device. Session parameters are stored for the WebView session, including session restoration by the client. Projects remain between launches until deleted or storage is cleared. Deleting one project may leave other projects, the local account scope code, settings and cached resources. These may stay on the device until the relevant app data is cleared.
  • Network information. The server needs it to process the connection. The statistics service keeps no log of individual openings. Daily and two-hour totals may be retained to compare periods. An individual opening cannot technically be removed from those totals.
  • Additional statistics. Your choice applies until you withdraw it, the study ends or 31 March 2027 ends in Moscow time, whichever comes first. Withdrawal stops additional counting on the device. You manage the choice separately on another device. A particular person’s opening cannot be identified in the aggregate count already recorded.
  • Correspondence. An ordinary inquiry is kept until the operator responds and for up to 30 additional days for follow-up. If an inquiry concerns a dispute or a legal obligation, the operator keeps the relevant portion until that purpose has been fulfilled or the mandatory retention period ends. This also applies to information needed to prove that your request was fulfilled.

8. Your rights and how to make a request

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed and to request details of the data, its source, purposes, retention periods and recipients. Where you have legal grounds, you may demand correction, blocking or destruction. You may also withdraw consent. Email boumrz@gmail.com and specify the version and type of data concerned. You can start with an ordinary email. If access to personal information requires proof of identity or a representative’s authority, the operator will explain what information is required and how to provide it. Formal requests are subject to Article 14(3) of Federal Law No. 152-FZ.

The operator provides the information or a reasoned refusal within 10 business days. The period may be extended by up to 5 business days with a reasoned notice. Confirmed inaccuracies are corrected within 7 business days of receiving confirmation. If consent is withdrawn and there is no other legal basis, the data is destroyed within up to 30 days in the cases provided for by Article 21 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ. A request to stop processing has a separate deadline of up to 10 business days, with a possible reasoned extension of up to 5 business days. You can appeal the operator’s actions to Roskomnadzor or to a court.

Deleting a project on the device, stopping future opening counts and requesting destruction of data already sent are separate actions. If your question concerns a project stored only on your device, the operator can explain where to manage it. When reviewing a request about Metrica events, the operator may ask for proportionate proof of account ownership.
