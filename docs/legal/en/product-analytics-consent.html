How to decline or withdraw consent

This choice is off by default. I can withdraw consent in the same settings section or turn off all analytics. This stops new requests on this device. Consent applies until I withdraw it or the measurement ends. The app asks separately for permission for a new purpose or edition of the form. Editing, saving and transferring projects remain available with either choice.

An individual launch or action cannot be identified in a total already collected. I can email the operator with questions about processing or to exercise my rights.
