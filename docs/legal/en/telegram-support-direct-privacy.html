Telegram Support Data

Edition 2026-11-24.2

This notice describes how reports submitted through /support in a private chat with @InteriorsEditorBot are processed. The InteriorsEditor operator is Roman Gennadyevich Zheltov.

What the operator receives

After you enter /support, the bot explains how to submit a report. When you send a description using Telegram’s send button, the operator receives it and any screenshots you already attached in a private chat with the bot. Once you receive a report number, you can add screenshots within 15 minutes or close photo submission with /cancel. The bot uses the report number, chat and message identifiers, timing and delivery status to deliver the report to the operator and return their reply to your chat. The service forwards screenshots using Telegram file identifiers.

Your report contains the text and images you choose to send to the bot from the chat. Project data, backups, editor launch parameters and analytics are outside this channel. Check screenshots before sending and hide addresses, personal data, passwords and access codes.

How long data is kept

The service deletes a draft from the server when you cancel it or after 24 hours of inactivity. Text and photo identifiers stay in the delivery queue while needed for delivery. After successful delivery, the service keeps the report number, the identifiers needed to reply and the status. Report data is retained on the server for up to 30 days after the last activity on the report.

Messages in your chat with the bot and copies in the operator’s private chat are stored by Telegram under its rules. Records on the operator’s server and messages in chats have separate retention periods. Telegram Privacy Policy.

How to contact the operator

To ask about the processing of your report, request a correction or ask the operator to delete information and copies of messages, email boumrz@gmail.com. Include the report number if you have it.
